A little about me

Approach & background.

I'm a Master's student studying Human-Computer Interaction in Chicago at DePaul University, with an interest in becoming a UX researcher. My background spans interactive art, interaction design, creative technology, and visual design — which means I tend to approach problems from several disciplines at once, rather than settling into one lane.

What ties the work together is a curiosity about people: how they make sense of new technologies, where systems fail them, and what it takes to design something that actually earns their trust. I move between structured research and hands-on making, often within the same project.

Outside of UX

I love the ocean, the mountains, and spending time outdoors. I love hiking or walking for hours in the city, exploring and enjoying the scenes along the path.
