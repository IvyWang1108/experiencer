Somatic City · Hackathon

What if you could see your heartbeat inside the city?

Somatic City transforms biosensing data and human gestures into an interactive visualization of Chicago—exploring the idea that people are the pulse that keeps a city alive.

  • Interactive Installation
  • Biosensing
  • TouchDesigner
  • Arduino

01 / Concept

People are the pulse of a city.

Movement, presence, and biological rhythms keep Chicago alive. The installation reframes the city as a living system continuously shaped and energized by its people.

04 / Biosensing

Becoming the pulse of Chicago

The live heartbeat is captured through Arduino and translated into a waveform in TouchDesigner. It appears behind the floating city model—not simply as BPM, but as part of the environment.

The participant is no longer only observing Chicago. Their body becomes part of the visualization.
